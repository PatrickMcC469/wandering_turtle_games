## 2. Games

## What we've made

### DinoLife: Prehistoric Survival

Pick a dinosaur — T-Rex, Velociraptor, Triceratops, and more — and guide it from hatchling to elder. Every season brings new challenges in a turn-based survival game you can play one turn at a time.

*Free on the App Store. iPhone and iPad.*

→ App Store badge → https://apps.apple.com/us/app/dinolife-prehistoric-survival/id6783614616

### Adventurers Vault

A coin purse for tabletop RPGs. Track copper, silver, gold, and platinum across every campaign, with automatic conversion, a full transaction history, and coins that tumble when you tilt your phone.

*Free on the App Store and Google Play. iPhone, iPad, Android, Mac, and Apple Vision.*

→ App Store badge → https://apps.apple.com/us/app/adventurers-vault/id6778613726

## Things I chose not to include, and why

- **A tagline about "indie"** — everyone says it. "Small games, made with care" says the same thing without the label.
- **"Follow us" / social icons** — none exist. Add when they do.
- **A bio or photo of Patrick** — this page is about the studio and the games. A person-centered "about" can come later if clients want it.
- **Star ratings or download counts** — they change, and they're not the point.
- **"Coming soon" dates** — games slip. "In development" is always true.
