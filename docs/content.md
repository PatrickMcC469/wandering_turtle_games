## Things I chose not to include, and why

- **A tagline about "indie"** — everyone says it. "Small games, made with care" says the same thing without the label.
- **"Follow us" / social icons** — none exist. Add when they do.
- **A bio or photo of Patrick** — this page is about the studio and the games. A person-centered "about" can come later if clients want it.
- **Star ratings or download counts** — they change, and they're not the point.
- **"Coming soon" dates** — games slip. "In development" is always true.
